### Adversarial checks

- **Time zones.** Engine dates are strings with UTC arithmetic; day 0, delays and CARs did not move
  between UTC−7 and UTC+14. Defect: the "Retrieved on" default used the UTC date (fixed, e2e).
- **Weekend / after-last-session publication.** Saturday 2023-07-08 anchors on Monday 2023-07-10;
  publication on the last session is `NOT_YET_TRADABLE` (unit test).
- **Missing, duplicated, unsorted observations.** Missing values stay `null` and make neighbouring
  returns undefined; duplicate dates are refused with the date; unsorted files are sorted.
- **No look-ahead.** Tried to construct a path where a rule or study reads a later date. Event
  conditions only see anchors `<= t`; target-series conditions only `<= t`. The weak spot was
  cross-market alignment: a prediction-market probability dated on the signal day was used at that
  day's close, although a 24/7 market's daily value can be recorded after the target closes. Fixed
  (only values dated before the signal; spec 00 rule 3 updated; unit test).
- **Decimals.** `probability_pct` 7 → 0.07, `1e1` → 0.1, `-0` probability accepted as 0, `-0` price
  refused, `"1,5"` refused with its line number. Extreme prices (1e-300 then 1e300) produced an
  infinite return that poisoned aggregates; now undefined (unit test).
- **Indicator references.** Wilder RSI 14 (72.9839, 68.8012), RSI 2 on a 5-point series (100, 50,
  75), rolling volatility (0.115470), volume z (5.809475) and CAR on a drifting series (+4 % on day
  0 over a 1 % baseline) were computed independently (by hand and in Python) and are pinned in
  `tests/acceptance.test.ts`. The engine matched before any change.
- **Backtest honesty.** Train/test split by entry date, cost on both legs, one position at a time
  with skipped signals counted, "very few trades" and "short period" badges, buy-and-hold shown.
  Noted, not changed: a period with no trades shows compounded 0 % (now in known limitations).
- **CSV formula injection.** Data cells were guarded but **comment lines were not**: a source such
  as `Vendor,=HYPERLINK(...)` produced a comment line whose second spreadsheet cell was a live
  formula; the rule name in the hits and trades comments had the same hole. Fixed: comment lines
  are one cell, and the guard also covers leading whitespace and full-width signs (unit and e2e).
- **Crafted workspace JSON.** `__proto__` keys, wrong schema or version, decreasing dates, volumes
  above 2^53 and duplicate ids are rejected with paths. But the CSV importer accepted a volume of
  `1e300` that the workspace schema rejects, so the automatic save became "unreadable" on the next
  load. Fixed; a unit test now checks that an edge-of-limits CSV import validates as a workspace.
- **localStorage corruption.** Invalid JSON and schema failures are kept under a `.corrupt` key,
  reported and offered as a download (existing e2e test).
- **Large inputs.** Limits (5 MB, 20,000 rows, 2,000 events, 20 series) are enforced in code; not
  load-tested at the limit in the browser.

### What the unit tests cover

- Dates: ISO validation with leap years, day arithmetic, "first session strictly after" search.
- Calculations against hand-computed references: returns with missing prices, compounding, sample sd,
  median, Wilder RSI (all gains 100, all losses 0, flat 50), volume z-score from prior sessions only,
  probability change in percentage points, Pearson (±1, constant input), lagged correlation peaking
  at the true lag, sample-size badges.
- Information time: `LookaheadError` on future inputs and on entry not after the signal; day 0
  anchoring.
- Sample market: deterministic across runs, labelled sample, weekdays only.
- Journey 2 in the engine: `disclosure-1` (31-day delay) anchors after publication and its post CAR is
  under 3 %, while the same event treated as published on the transaction date shows over 8 %.
- Event study: inclusion, n and badges, OUT_OF_RANGE and NOT_YET_TRADABLE exclusions, overlap flags,
  CAR paths consistent with pre and post CAR.
- Rules: two-evidence-type requirement, price target, missing probability series; signals only after
  publication; entries strictly after signals; a disclosure rule never fires before day 0.
- Backtest: train/test assignment by entry date, cost on both legs, no overlapping positions, cost
  clamped to 0..500 bps, small-sample badges.
- CSV: RFC 4180 parsing (quotes, doubled quotes, CRLF, BOM, multi-line fields, line numbers),
  all-or-nothing imports with line errors, duplicate dates, multi-symbol files, `probability_pct`,
  event validation (publication before event, buy on a catalyst, duplicate ids), formula guard.
- Workspace: round trip; rejects unknown keys, wrong version, bad JSON, duplicate ids, decreasing
  dates, probabilities outside 0..1, publication before the event.
